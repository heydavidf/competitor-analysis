# Fictional food-swap example

Everything here is invented for demonstration. GatherSlot, coordinator Mira, North Quay Swap, and every named competitor are fictional. The fixture notes are synthetic inputs, not observations of real sites or customer interviews. The sample verdict and grades illustrate the skill's output format; they are not market findings.

**Decision frame:** A fictional volunteer coordinator needs to fill three Saturday pickup shifts without double-booking a volunteer. GatherSlot proposes a one-screen shift assignment view with an availability check. The simulated market is English-language neighborhood food swaps. The example uses rapid mode with two direct and two indirect alternatives.

The `fixtures/` folder contains invented product and customer notes. `research-matrix.csv` is the sample evidence record, `competitive-analysis-brief.md` the sample decision, and `dashboard.html` the offline view. Open the dashboard in a browser and use its type and subgroup filters. Its source links open the local fixture notes when your browser permits local file navigation.

To exercise the skill afresh, give your agent this folder's decision frame and fixture notes, ask it to use only those notes, and direct it to write a new output folder outside this example. It should mark every conclusion as a simulation. For a live study, supply real customer context and let the agent collect dated sources.
